Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026. Contact: info@edgelings.com

Edgelings is operated by Matej Mesko, Slovenia, who is the data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This policy covers the Edgelings game for iPhone, iPad and Android and the website edgelings.com.

In short

  • You play without an email address or a password. The game creates an anonymous player account for you.
  • We store your player name, progress, coins, scores and purchases, so the game works and the rankings are fair.
  • Your player name and scores are visible to other players in the rankings.
  • Usage statistics and personalised ads run only if you accept. The game asks you after level 3.
  • We never sell your data. You can delete your account in the game at any time.

What we collect

Player account. The first time the game goes online, it creates an anonymous account with a random player number. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Player name. A random name the game picks for you, or a name you choose. Names are checked against a list of blocked words and must be unique.

Game data. Your progress through levels and tiers, scores, daily table tries and the shots you played in them, your weekly league, coins, items, collection and settings. The shots let our server replay a daily table try and check the score is real.

Purchases. Purchases go through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive a record of what you bought, linked to your random player number, so we can deliver items and restore them. This record is processed by RevenueCat on our behalf.

Technical data. When the game or the website connects to a server, the server receives your IP address. Our providers use it to deliver the service and keep it secure. We do not store it in your player account.

On your phone. Your progress and settings are also saved on your phone, so the game works offline.

Why we use your data, and on what legal basis

  • Running the game, saving progress, rankings, leagues and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Fair play and security, such as replaying daily table scores, blocking offensive names and limiting fake accounts: our legitimate interest in a fair and safe game (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Usage statistics: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given in the game.
  • Personalised advertising: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), given in the game.
  • Answering your emails: our legitimate interest in helping you (Article 6(1)(f)).

Rankings

The Daily, Weekly and All time rankings show your player name, score, position and league to all players. Do not use your real name as your player name if you do not want others to see it.

Advertising

Edgelings is free because of ads, shown through Google AdMob. A banner appears on menu screens, never during a level. You can also choose to watch a rewarded ad, for example for an extra try or coins. No ads are shown before you answer the consent question after level 3.

If you accept, ads may be personalised. Your device advertising identifier is then shared with Google and its advertising partners, with approximate location and information about how you use the game. In the European Union and the United Kingdom, Google may show its own consent form as well. On iPhone and iPad you also see Apple's App Tracking Transparency question.

If you decline, ads are not personalised. Google still processes limited data, for example to count ads and prevent fraud. No game feature is held back.

Changing your mind. In the game, open Settings and tap the Ads consent button. On iPhone you can also go to Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Tracking. On Android, go to Settings, then Google, then Ads.

Buying Remove ads removes the banner. Rewarded ads stay available if you want them. Google acts as an independent controller for the advertising data it receives, under Google's own privacy policy.

Usage statistics

If you accept, the game sends usage statistics to PostHog, hosted in the European Union. For example, we count which levels are started and finished, scores, coins earned and spent, and purchases. These events are linked to your random player number, never to a name or email address. If you decline, or switch Ads consent to no in Settings, no statistics are sent.

The website does not use cookies, does not run statistics and does not store anything on your device.

Notifications

Reminders about the daily table, rankings and chests are scheduled on your phone, at most 3 a week. You can switch each of them off in Settings or in your phone settings.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase: player accounts, game data and rankings.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records.
  • PostHog: usage statistics, only with your consent.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • Apple and Google: app stores and payments, under their own privacy policies.
  • GitHub: hosting of this website.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

Your player account and game data are stored with Supabase. Usage statistics are stored with PostHog in the European Union. Some providers, such as RevenueCat, Google and GitHub, process data in the United States. They use the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework to protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your player account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed straight away, and from backups within 30 days. Records of purchases are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as tax and accounting law requires. Usage statistics are kept for up to 12 months. Emails to us are kept for as long as needed to answer them, and no longer than 2 years.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and move your data, and to object to how we use it. You can withdraw any consent at any time.

  • Change your player name: in Settings.
  • Delete everything: in Settings, tap Delete my account. If you no longer have the game, see how to delete your account.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us your player name and we send it within 30 days.

You also have the right to complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec), or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Children

Edgelings is not designed for children under 13. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, ask a parent or carer before you accept personalised ads and statistics. If you believe a child under 13 uses the game, email us and we delete the account.

Changes

We update this page when the policy changes and show the new effective date at the top. Important changes are shown in the game.
