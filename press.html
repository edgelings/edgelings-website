Short description

Flick glossy slimes across the table. The closer they stop to the edge, the more they score. Over the edge scores nothing.

Long description

Edgelings is a casual game about stopping at exactly the right moment. You pull a slime back in a sling and let it go across a table. A slime near the far edge scores up to 3 times the points. A slime that goes over the edge scores nothing.

2 slimes of the same kind merge into a bigger one, from little Blip up to King Plum. Later tables add honey, ice, sand, trampolines, pegs, wind and special slimes such as Magnet, Ghost, Heavy and Sparkler.

There are 1,200 levels in 6 tiers, from Easy to Crazy. A daily table gives every player in the world the same challenge, and weekly leagues move players between Bronze, Silver and Gold. The whole game plays offline.
