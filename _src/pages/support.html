Delete your account

In the game: open Settings, tap Delete my account, then Delete everything. Your player name, progress, coins and rankings are removed for good. Your phone must be online for this.

Without the game: if you no longer have the game installed, email info@edgelings.com with the subject "Delete my Edgelings account" and your player name. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting the account does not cancel or refund purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google, see below.

Frequently asked questions

Is Edgelings free?

Yes. The game is free and paid for by ads. You can buy coins, a starter pack or remove the banner ad, but you never need to.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All levels play offline. The daily table, rankings and leagues need a connection. Your results are sent once you are back online.

How do I change my player name?

Open Settings, type a new name and tap Save, or tap Random. The first change is free. Later changes cost coins. Names need 3 to 12 letters, digits or underscores, and must not be taken.

I got a new phone. Is my progress lost?

Your player account is anonymous and linked to the game on your phone. Moving it to a new phone is not possible yet. Purchases of Remove ads come back with Restore purchases on the new phone, using the same Apple or Google account.

A purchase did not arrive

Make sure you are online, then open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, email us with your player name and the order number from Apple or Google.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments and subscriptions, then Budget and history.

How do I change my ads choice?

Open Settings and tap the Ads consent button. Yes means ads and statistics may use your data. No means ads are not personalised and statistics are off.

I saw an offensive player name

Email us the name. We remove names that break the Terms and Conditions.
