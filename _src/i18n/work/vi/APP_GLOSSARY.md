# Edgelings: Vietnamese (vi) glossary

## Register rule
- The player is addressed as **bạn** (neutral, polite, no gender) in sentences. The game speaks as **chúng tôi** in the consent and notification texts.
- Share texts use **tôi / bạn** ("Bạn vượt được tôi không?").
- Buttons, hints, missions and tasks use the bare verb or a noun, as Vietnamese games do ("Chơi", "Chốt", "Lưu", "Thắng {n} màn"). "Hãy" is added only in full sentences that ask the player to do something.
- Switch buttons show the state after a colon: "Nhạc: bật", "{thing}: tắt".
- Vietnamese has 1 plural form (other). No classifier is used after {n} ("{n} slime", "{n} xu", "{n} màn").
- Full diacritics, precomposed (NFC). No exclamation mark unless English has one, no dashes, no semicolons, digits for numbers.
- Slime names always follow the noun "slime" or a colon, so they need no ending: "Tạo slime {slime}", "Lớn nhất: {slime}".
- {world} and {league} follow their noun in basic form: "thế giới {world}", "hạng {league}".
- {price} and {coins} arrive as "150 xu" and stand after a colon or after "giá", "có".
