# Edgelings: German (de) glossary

## Register rule
- Formal address with **Sie / Ihr** wherever the player is addressed directly (notifications, consent, shop, settings).
- Buttons, hints, missions and tasks use the **infinitive** or a noun, as German games do ("Sichern", "Speichern", "Slime zurückziehen und loslassen", "{n} Level gewinnen"). This also keeps texts short.
- Share texts avoid addressing the reader ("Wer schlägt mich?"), so no du/Sie choice is needed there.
- Neutral German for DE/AT/CH, with ß. No gendered player nouns ("für alle" instead of "für jeden Spieler").
- No exclamation mark unless English has one, no dashes, no semicolons. Hyphens only inside compounds ("Gratis-Truhe", "{league}-Liga").
