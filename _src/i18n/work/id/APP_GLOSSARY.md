# Edgelings, Indonesian (id) glossary

## Register
- Player addressed as "Anda" in sentences. Buttons use the short bare verb or noun that Indonesian games use (Main, Simpan, Kembali, Amankan).
- Exception: the 2 share texts ("Can you beat me?") are the player speaking to friends, so they use "aku" and no "Anda".
- No gender marking needed in Indonesian. No exclamation marks beyond the English ones, no dashes, no semicolons, Western digits.
- Nouns after a number stay singular (5 jaring, 2 slime). Only plural form needed: other.
- Slime names always follow the noun "slime" (slime {slime}), worlds follow "dunia", leagues follow "liga".
