# Edgelings glossary: pt (Portuguese of Portugal)

## Register rule
- Formal address without "tu" and without the pronoun "você": 3rd person singular verbs and "o seu / a sua" (Puxe, Garanta, Tem {coins}, A sua liga).
- Buttons and missions use the infinitive (Jogar, Guardar, Voltar, Fundir {n} vezes).
- Ongoing actions use the European form "a + infinitive" (A carregar o ranking), never the Brazilian gerund.
- Player gender is never marked. Participles that appear agree with the slime (masculine) or with a noun in the text, not with the player.
- European spelling and vocabulary: telemóvel, ecrã, Definições, mealheiro, prémio/bónus, Íman, partilhar, equipa, em direto.
- No exclamation mark unless English has one, no dashes, no semicolons.
