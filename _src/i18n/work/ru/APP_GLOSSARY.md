# Edgelings, Russian (ru) glossary

## Register and style rules applied
- Address: вы in lower case, plural imperative in hints, missions and messages (Оттяните, Заберите, Попробуйте).
- Buttons: infinitive or short noun (Играть, Забрать, Сохранить, Назад, Дальше, Домой).
- Player gender unknown: no singular past-tense verbs or gendered adjectives about the player. Impersonal or present-tense forms instead (Вы переходите в лигу, Откройте счёт, Кто наберёт больше?).
- The letter ё is written everywhere (звёзд, ещё, Лёгкий).
- {price} and {coins} arrive in the nominative ("150 монет", "1 монета"), so they stand only after a colon or after "У вас".
- {world} and {league} stand in the basic form inside «» after the nouns мир and лига, or after a colon.
- {slime} always follows the noun слайм or a colon, so the English name needs no ending.
- "слайм" is treated as an animate masculine noun (accusative: слайма, слаймов).
- No exclamation marks beyond the English ones, no dashes, no semicolons. "Топ-25" and "Топ-100" use a plain hyphen.
- Plural "other" form (fractions only, never shown because {n} is whole) uses the genitive singular.
