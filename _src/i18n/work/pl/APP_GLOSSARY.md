# Edgelings, Polish (pl) glossary

## Register rule
- No Pan or Pani, no "Twój/Twoja", no second person in full sentences. Messages, notifications, settings and the consent text use impersonal or nominal forms ("Można to zmienić", "Saldo: {coins}", "Nazwa gracza") and "Proszę + infinitive" for requests ("Proszę spróbować później").
- Buttons, mission lines and the short play hints use the singular imperative, which is the standard form on Polish game buttons (Graj, Zapisz, Zgarnij, Odciągnij glutka i puść).
- Consent buttons are first person: Akceptuję, Odrzucam.
- The game speaks as "we" where it speaks at all (Dziękujemy, Damy znać).
- Player gender is never marked. Masculine forms refer only to "glutek" (Uwolniony!, Największy).
- {price} and {coins} always stand after a colon or at the start, so the ready phrase stays in the nominative. {slime}, {world}, {league} stand after a noun or a colon and are never declined.
