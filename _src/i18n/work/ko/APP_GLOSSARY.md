# Edgelings: Korean (ko) glossary

## Register and style rules applied
- Sentences: 해요체 throughout (…해요, …하세요, …돼요). No 합쇼체, no 반말. "Thank you" is 감사해요 to stay consistent.
- Buttons, tabs, labels: short nouns or -기 forms (플레이, 챙기기, 무료 상자 열기, 저장). Mission and star-goal lines: -기 form (슬라임 잃지 않기).
- Status and confirmations: noun endings (… 완료, … 중, 켜짐 / 꺼짐).
- The player is never addressed with a pronoun, except 귀하 in the legal consent question. "Your" is 내 on labels (내 리그, 내 이름).
- Particles: no 은/는, 이/가, 을/를, (으)로 directly after a placeholder. Placeholders stand after a colon, before a noun (… 리그, … 월드), or before a fixed counter or invariant particle (점, 번, 개, 마리, 부터, 시간, 분). The only particle after a Latin name is the fixed "Edgelings는" and "Edgelings에서".
- {price} and {coins} arrive as "150코인" and always stand after a colon or at the start of a list.
- Counters: slimes 마리, items, levels and stars 개, times 번 or 회, points 점.
- Korean has 1 plural form (other).
- No exclamation marks, dashes or semicolons beyond the English. Digits stay Western.
