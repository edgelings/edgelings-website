# Edgelings: Slovenian (sl) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences, hints, missions, notifications and the consent text use **vikanje** (2nd person plural: "Povlecite", "Odprite", "Bodite prvi"). The masculine plural of vikanje is the generic polite form and is used for a player of unknown gender ("Napredovali ste").
- Buttons use the short singular imperative or a noun, as Slovenian apps and games do on buttons ("Igraj", "Unovči", "Shrani", "Sprejmi", "Odpri zdaj"). Where a button would address the player reflexively, a noun is used instead ("Ponovi: ogled oglasa", not "oglej si").
- No gendered past participles for the player. Counters and states are neuter or impersonal ("uporabljeno {n}x", "še neodkrito").
- {coins} and {price} always stand in the nominative, after a colon ("Stanje: {coins}", "+2 sluzka: {price}"), because "3 kovanci" would be wrong after a verb or preposition that needs the accusative ("3 kovance").
- {slime} always follows the noun "sluzek" or stands in the nominative, so the name needs no ending.
- {world} follows "svet" or a colon. {league} is a feminine adjective placed directly before "liga" in the nominative or after a colon.
- Percent is written the Slovenian way with a non-breaking space (U+00A0) before the sign: "+10 %".
- Plurals: one, two (dual), few (3 and 4), other (5 and more, genitive plural).
