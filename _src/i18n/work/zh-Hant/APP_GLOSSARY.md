# Edgelings: Traditional Chinese (zh-Hant, Taiwan and Hong Kong) glossary

## Register and style rules
- **您** wherever the player is addressed in a sentence (notifications, consent, shop, settings, account deletion). Never 你 toward the player.
- Exception: the 2 share texts ("Can you beat me?", "I scored {score} in Edgelings. Can you beat me?") use **你**. There the player is speaking to a friend, and 您 would sound stiff and odd between friends.
- Buttons, hints, missions and labels: short verb or noun with no pronoun (收分, 儲存, 返回, 通過{n}關).
- Taiwan vocabulary that Hong Kong readers also understand: 設定, 儲存, 載入, 離線, 帳號, 隱私權政策, 應用程式商店, 造型, 圖鑑, 存錢筒, 迪斯可, 暱稱, 字元, 復原.
- Full-width punctuation ，。：？！（）「」. The slash in "{score} / {goal}" and the symbols +, %, x, = stay half-width.
- No space between Western digits or number placeholders and Chinese characters (第{n}關, {n}金幣, 3次機會). 1 space around Latin-letter words and around {slime} and {time} when they touch Chinese characters (合出 {slime}, 為 Edgelings 評分), because the slime names are Latin and "King Plum" contains a space.
- {world} sits inside 「」 in sentences, or after a colon, so the label stays in its basic form. {league} stands directly before 聯賽 ({league}聯賽).
- No exclamation mark unless English has one. No dashes, no semicolons. Western digits only.
- Player gender never expressed (Chinese needs no gendered forms here).
