# Edgelings 日本語 (ja) glossary

## Register and script rules applied
- Sentences: です/ます polite style. Player addressed as あなた only in the consent text, otherwise no pronoun.
- Buttons, labels, mission lines: short noun or dictionary form (保存, 戻る, 同意する, {n}回合体する).
- Hints: 〜ましょう form. Floating shouts: short, energetic, ！ only where English has "!".
- Share texts ("Can you beat me?") are the player's own words to friends, so casual (〜かな？, 〜出したよ).
- Greeting: {name}さん.
- Full-width punctuation 。、！？：（）「」. Western digits. +, %, x, / kept. No dashes, no semicolons, no wave dash.
- Counters: slimes 匹, items 個, times 回. {price} and {coins} arrive as "150コイン" and always follow ： or stand at sentence start.
- {world} is wrapped in 「」 inside sentences or follows ：. {league} is followed directly by リーグ.
- Edgelings and Blip, Mossy, Sunny, Pomme, King Plum stay in Latin letters.
