# Edgelings: Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans) glossary

## Register and script rules
- **您 / 您的** wherever the player is addressed in a sentence (notifications, consent, settings, shop, account). Buttons, hints, missions and shouts are short verb or noun phrases without a pronoun, as Chinese games do.
- Share texts avoid addressing the reader ("谁能赢过我？"), so no 你/您 choice is needed there (same solution as de/fr in round 1).
- Mainland-neutral simplified usage, readable in Singapore and Malaysia (账号, 应用商店, 昵称, 皮肤).
- Full-width punctuation (，。：？！（）、). "！" only where English has "!". No dashes, no semicolons. English texts without a final full stop have none in Chinese either.
- Western digits everywhere. +, %, x, /, = kept ("+10%", "（周末 x2）").
- Spacing: 1 half-width space between Chinese characters and a number or a Latin name ({n}, {score}, {slime}, {name}, {time}, and {price}/{coins}, which start with a digit). No space after full-width punctuation. No space around {world}, {league}, {thing}, which are Chinese labels.
- Measure words: 只 for slimes, 张 for nets, 个 for aim helpers, 款 for skins, 颗 for stars, 关 for levels, 次 for tries and merges. Coins take no measure word ("{n} 金币") so the phrase also fits after "+" and after a colon.
- Plural: only the form "other".
