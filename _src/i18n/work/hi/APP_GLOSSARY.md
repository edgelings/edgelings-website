# Edgelings: Hindi (hi) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences, hints, missions, notifications and the consent text use the polite **आप** register: imperatives in the -एँ / -ें form ("खेलें", "खोलें", "बनाएँ"), possessives "आपका / आपकी".
- Buttons use the same short polite imperative ("खेलें", "सेव करें", "पक्का करें") or a bare noun ("वापस", "होम", "शॉप"), as Hindi mobile games do.
- Player gender is unknown. No gendered verb ending refers to the player: no "आप सकते हैं", "आप गए", "अटक गए". Passive or impersonal wording is used instead ("बदला जा सकता है", "स्किप किया जा सकता है", "आपको सूचना मिलेगी", "अब आप {league} लीग में हैं"). Gendered endings only agree with things: स्लाइम (masculine), टेबल, लीग, दुनिया, गुल्लक, फुलझड़ी, कोशिश (feminine), संदूक (masculine).
- Everyday Hindi with the English loanwords Indian players expect, in Devanagari: लेवल, कॉइन, पॉइंट, स्टार, स्कोर, मर्ज, बोनस, रिस्क, स्किन, शॉप, रैंकिंग, लीग, डेली, वीकली.
- "Edgelings" and the 5 slime names stay in Latin letters. {slime} is treated as masculine and takes no ending ("{slime} बनाएँ", "यह तो {slime} था!").
- {price} and {coins} stand after a colon, unchanged ("आपके पास: {coins}", "+2 स्लाइम: {price}"). The coin phrase is "{n} कॉइन" in both plural forms.
- {world} is an uninflected label placed before "दुनिया" ("{world} दुनिया") or after a colon. {league} stands before "लीग" or after a colon.
- Plurals: one (0 and 1) and other. {n} is kept in every form, because "one" also covers 0 in Hindi.
- Punctuation: danda (।) as the full stop inside multi-sentence texts, no dashes, no semicolons, exclamation mark only where English has one. Western digits. "x" and "%" kept ("{n}x मर्ज", "+10%").
- Spelling: nukta on ज़, फ़, ख़ (ज़ोन, मुफ़्त, ख़रीदारी), written as base letter plus nukta (NFC). No nukta on क (करीब, किस्म, कीमत). Chandrabindu in -एँ forms (बनाएँ, जाएँ), यहाँ, हाँ.
