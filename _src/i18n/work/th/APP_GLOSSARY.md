# Edgelings: Thai (th) glossary

## Register and style rules applied
- Polite neutral written Thai. No gendered particles (ครับ, ค่ะ, คะ) anywhere. The player is addressed as คุณ, and only where a pronoun is needed.
- First person is avoided on the share card (no ฉัน, ผม, เรา), so the sender's gender is never implied. Exception: the button "ลบบัญชีของฉัน", where ฉัน is the neutral app convention.
- Thai punctuation: no full stops, sentences are separated by 1 space. No question marks, questions end with ไหม or หรือไม่. Exclamation marks only where English has one. No dashes, semicolons, emojis or ZWSP.
- Western digits with a space on both sides of a number ("เล่นได้ 3 ครั้ง"). Classifiers follow the number: ตัว (slimes), ผืน (nets), ชิ้น (aim helper), ดวง (stars), ชุด (skin), ด่าน (levels), ครั้ง (tries, times).
- Thai has 1 plural form: only "other" is given.
- {world} and {league} are attached straight after the head noun in their basic form: โลก{world} (โลกยาก), ลีก{league} (ลีกทอง). Otherwise they stand after a colon.
- {price} and {coins} ("150 เหรียญ") stand after a colon or after คุณมี / ราคา, unchanged.
- Edgelings and the slime names (Blip, Mossy, Sunny, Pomme, King Plum) stay in Latin letters.
