# Edgelings glossary: Dutch (nl, neutral for the Netherlands and Flanders)

## Register rule
- Formal address: "u" and "uw" everywhere ("U hebt", not "U heeft" and not "je").
- Buttons: infinitive, as Dutch games do ("Spelen", "Opslaan", "Opgeven", "Pakken"). A button with a value uses the short stem ("Pak {score}").
- Missions, hints and goals: bare imperative stem, which is neutral between u and je ("Win {n} levels", "Speel de dagtafel").
- Share texts avoid direct address ("Wie verslaat mij?") so the player does not address friends with "u".
- No guess at the player's gender. No exclamation mark unless English has one. No dashes, no semicolons.
- Ad is always "reclame", watching an ad is "reclame kijken".
