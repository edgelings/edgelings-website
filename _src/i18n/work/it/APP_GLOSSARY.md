# Edgelings, Italian (it) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences that address the player use the formal **Lei** (verbs in the 3rd person: "Tiri indietro lo slime", "Riprovi più tardi", "Può saltare questo livello"). Pronouns and possessives are lower case (la, lei, suo, sua).
- Buttons use the standard Italian app and game button form ("Gioca", "Salva", "Incassa", "Riprova", "Apri ora", "Elimina tutto") or a noun ("Indietro", "Rinuncia", "Negozio"). "Accept" and "Decline" are the 1st person "Accetto" and "Rifiuto".
- Missions and goals use the infinitive ("Vincere {n} livelli", "Fondere {n} volte", "Creare {slime}").
- Buttons that mention an ad avoid a verb of address: "Riprova con un annuncio", "Salta con un annuncio", "Annuncio: +1 slime".
- The player's gender is never marked. Masculine participles ("Salvato", "Libero", "usato", "fuso") refer to the slime (lo slime), never to the player.
- Plural categories written: one, many, other (many = other).
