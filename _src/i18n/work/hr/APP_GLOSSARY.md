# Edgelings: hrvatski (hr) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences addressed to the player use the polite Vi form (2nd person plural verbs: "Povucite", "Osigurajte", "Imate", "Prešli ste"). Pronouns are written lowercase (vaš, vam), as in Croatian apps, and avoided where possible.
- Short action buttons use the conventional Croatian software imperative (Igraj, Spremi, Osiguraj, Odustani, Natrag, Ponovi, Preskoči, Otvori, Izbriši). This is the standard button form in Croatian apps and games and is not read as informal address.
- Consent buttons use the 1st person (Prihvaćam, Odbijam). "Delete your account?" uses the infinitive (Izbrisati račun?).
- Missions and hints are sentences, so they use Vi (Riješite, Spojite, Osvojite, Odigrajte).
- Gender of the player is never marked. Polite plural (Prešli ste) is the neutral standard. "Be the first" became "Zauzmite prvo mjesto", "Can you beat me?" became the impersonal "Može li bolje?".
- Slime names follow the noun "sluzić" where a case is needed (Stvorite sluzića {slime}). {world} and {league} stand after a colon in basic form (svijet: {world}, liga: {league}). {price} and {coins} stand after a colon.
