# Edgelings, Turkish (tr) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences, hints, missions, notifications and every text with "your": polite **siz** forms (çekin, kazanın, sandığınız, liginiz).
- Short command buttons (1 to 3 words): the bare imperative that Turkish games and apps use on buttons (Oyna, Kaydet, Geri al, Tekrar dene, Pes et, Hesabımı sil). No "sen" pronoun or "sen" possessive appears anywhere.
- No suffix is ever attached to a {placeholder}, to a slime name or to "Edgelings". A noun carries the suffix instead ({slime} jölesini, {world} dünyasında, {league} lige, Edgelings oyununda, {n}. seviyeyi).
- Numbers after a count keep the noun singular (3 deneme, 2 jöle), so both plural forms (one, other) are the same except for the chest text.
- Percent sign before the number, as Turkish writes it: +%10, %20.
- {price} and {coins} always stand after a colon or at the start of a phrase.
- The share texts avoid addressing the friend: "Beni kim geçer?" (who can beat me?).
