# Edgelings es-419 (Latin American Spanish) glossary

Register: usted throughout in sentences ("Vuelva", "Asegure", "Tiene"). Buttons in the infinitive ("Jugar", "Guardar", "Asegurar"). No vosotros, no voseo, no tuteo. Player gender never assumed (no "lo espera", no "atascado"). Neutral vocabulary for Mexico to Argentina. Simple past instead of present perfect ("Subió", "Hice", "cerró").
