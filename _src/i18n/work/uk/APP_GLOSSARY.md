# Edgelings, Ukrainian (uk) glossary

## Register rule
- Sentences address the player as "ви" (lower case) with the polite plural imperative: Відтягніть, Спробуйте, Заберіть.
- Buttons and mission or star-goal lines use the infinitive, as Ukrainian games do: Грати, Зберегти, Забрати, Виграти {n} рівнів.
- The game speaks as "we": Дякуємо, Ми повідомимо, Завантажуємо.
- Player gender is never marked. No singular past tense or adjective about the player (so "Перше місце вільне" instead of "Будьте першим"). Past plural with "ви" (посіли, поділилися) is neutral. Masculine forms refer only to "слайм" (Найбільший, більшого).
- {price} and {coins} stand after a colon or after "У вас", where the nominative phrase fits for every number. {slime} stands after the noun "слайма" or a colon. {world} stands after "світ" / "у світі" or a colon, {league} after "ліга:". None is declined.
- {time} as a clock time stands after "Час появи:" to avoid the о/об choice. As a duration it stands after "через".
- Apostrophe is the plain ' (U+0027) so any font shows it. No «» quotes, no dashes, no semicolons.

## Plural forms
one (1, 21), few (2 to 4), many (0, 5 and more), other (fractions, genitive singular). The "one" form always keeps {n}, because it also serves 21, 31 and so on.
